A concise product specification guide, sourced from Apple's published technical information.

Two displays

7.6-inch 1878 × 2670 OLED inner display with nano-texture finish; 5.4-inch 1398 × 2034 OLED outer display; ProMotion up to 120Hz on both.

Foldable body

Grade 5 titanium foldable design with precision hinge; 5.2 mm thin open, 11.3 mm when closed; 254 g.

Chip

A20 Pro with 6-core CPU, 7-core GPU and dual 16-core Neural Engine.

Camera system

48MP Fusion Main, 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide, 12MP optical-quality 2x Telephoto mode; foldable camera modes including Duo Preview.

Battery

Up to 44 hours of video playback on outer display or 31 hours on inner display.

Storage

256GB, 512GB and 1TB listed on Apple's main tech specs; configuration availability may vary by region.

Finish

Night Sky and Star White.
